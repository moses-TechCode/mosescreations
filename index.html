How to Fix Airtel Zambia 4G With No Internet (APN Settings)

By Your Name November 24, 2026 2 min read

Ever seen the 4G icon on your phone but nothing loads? On Airtel Zambia, the most common cause is a missing APN, or access point name. Without it, your phone connects to the network but can’t get mobile data. I fixed it on my Tecno Spark 30 Pro in a few minutes, and here is how.

Steps

  1. Open Settings, then Mobile Network, then Access Point Names.
  2. Tap the + (add) button.
  3. Enter these details:
    • Name: Airtel Zambia
    • APN: internet
    • APN type: default (or default,mms for picture messaging)
    • APN protocol: IPv4 or IPv4/IPv6
  4. Leave everything else blank, including proxy, port, username, password, server and MMSC.
  5. Save, select the new Airtel Zambia APN, then restart your phone.
Menu names can differ between phones. If you can’t find Access Point Names, search for “APN” in your settings.

Still not working?

  • Check that mobile data is on, and that your SIM is selected for data if your phone has two.
  • Make sure you have an active data bundle.
  • Set your preferred network type to 4G/LTE, then turn airplane mode on and off.

Using your phone as a hotspot?

The same APN powers your hotspot, so once it’s set up, other devices can connect and get internet too. Hotspot speed is shared across every connected device, so videos may load slower than on your phone alone.
